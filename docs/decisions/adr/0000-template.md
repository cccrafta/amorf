## Context and Problem Statement

What forces the decision? Two to five sentences, in the form of a problem or a question. State constraints that exist whether or not this decision is made.

## Decision Drivers

- Driver 1: a quality, constraint or requirement that the options are judged by.
- Driver 2

## Considered Options

- Option 1
- Option 2
- Option 3

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "Option N", because it is the only option that meets the drivers that matter most (name them).

### Consequences

- Good: what becomes easier or possible.
- Bad: what becomes harder, and the cost accepted.
- Neutral: follow-up work this creates.

### Confirmation

Optional. How compliance is checked: a build step, a script, a review rule.

Optional. One short block per option, only where the trade-off is not obvious from the list above.
