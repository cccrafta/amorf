# DDR-0000: Short title in the imperative, stating the design decision

A design decision record (DDR) uses the MADR shape of an ADR, plus the fields above and the section "Structural / material logic". Use a DDR for design, spatial and material choices; use an ADR for technology choices. Rules: [../../conventions/decision-records.md](../../conventions/decision-records.md).

## Structural / material logic

How does the decision follow from structural necessity, material properties, layout logic or honest joinery, so that the structure is the ornament and nothing is added for looks alone? If the honest answer is that it does not, say so here, and record why the exception is accepted.

## Evidence

Experiments, sketches, references and observations that back the decision, each with a link. A decision with no evidence says so.
