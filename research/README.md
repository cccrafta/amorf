# Research

amorf is a design-research project with architects. The research folder holds the thinking behind the app: what the project believes, what it tried, and what it publishes. The app in `apps/web` is one output of this work, not the whole of it.

| Folder | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| [theory/](theory/README.md) | The manifesto, the hypotheses, and the [ontology](theory/ontology/README.md): the parts of the site (entity, field, coordinates, space, journey, archive), each with a hub page that collects its research and deductions. |
| [studies/](studies/) | One folder per study: desk research into a concept or precedent, with sources and deductions. Template: `studies/0000-template/`. |
| [experiments/](experiments/) | One folder per experiment: a question, a method, observations and an outcome. Template: `experiments/0000-template/`. |
| [portfolio/](portfolio/README.md) | Case studies assembled from studies, experiments and decision records; one part per ontology part, after an introductory part 0. |

## Flow

1. **Theory raises a question.** A hypothesis in `theory/` is not yet knowledge; it asks to be tested.
2. **A study or an experiment answers it.** A study finds out what is already known (precedents, theory); an experiment tests by making. An experiment is done when its outcome is filled in; a study when its deductions are. Abandoned is a valid end: record why it stopped, because a failed experiment and its trade-offs are part of the work (process over final artifact).
3. **The part hub compiles it.** Each item names its ontology `parts`; a deduction worth holding goes, dated and cited, into the Position of each part hub it concerns.
4. **A decision record commits.** When an outcome changes how the project is built or designed, a [DDR](../docs/decisions/ddr/0000-template.md) (design, spatial, material) or [ADR](../docs/decisions/adr/0000-template.md) (technology) records the decision and cites the experiment as evidence.

All research grouped by part: [INDEX.md](INDEX.md) (generated by `pnpm research:index`; data in `index.json`; script in [`../scripts/research-index.mjs`](../scripts/research-index.mjs)).

Rules for studies and experiments: [../docs/conventions/research-log.md](../docs/conventions/research-log.md). When to write which record: [../docs/conventions/decision-records.md](../docs/conventions/decision-records.md).

## What belongs here and what does not

Experiment media (photographs, film, drawings, models) goes to R2, like entity media, and is listed by key in the experiment's frontmatter. Never put media files in git. Research that concerns one entity belongs to that entity's `entity.json`, not here.
