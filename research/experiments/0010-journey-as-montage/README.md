# EXP-0010: Journey recorded and replayed as montage

Follows [STU-0001](../../studies/0001-promenade-architecturale/README.md). Builds on EXP-0006 to EXP-0009. Read Eisenstein's *Montage and Architecture* in full before detailing the method.

## Hypothesis

Eisenstein calls the walk around the Acropolis a montage "our legs create", and notes the reversal: in film the spectator sits still while the path moves, in architecture the spectator moves (STU-0001, notes on Eisenstein). The field sits between the two, and the ontology says a journey is itself a space ([journey](../../theory/ontology/journey.md)). Recording views, not only nodes, would let a journey be both: a film to watch and a route to walk.

It is wrong if the replay reads as jumps, or if a second visitor cannot follow it.
