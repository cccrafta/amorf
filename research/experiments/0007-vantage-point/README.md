# EXP-0007: A vantage point inside each space

Follows [STU-0001](../../studies/0001-promenade-architecturale/README.md). Depends on [EXP-0006](../0006-thresholds-between-spaces/README.md): a vantage point needs a space to look over.

## Hypothesis

Villa Savoye's ramp climbs to a roof terrace that closes the route and looks back over it; level design uses the same move, a high point that shows the whole island (STU-0001, notes on the houses and the digital line). If orientation is better gained from a place in the field than from a panel laid over it, the visitor never leaves the promenade to find their way.

It is wrong if a map overlay orients as well or better, or if the climb reads as a detour.
