# EXP-0014: One instanced rest cube with media on its faces, morphed to a sphere, on WebGPU and WebGL 2

The spike [STU-0006](../../studies/0006-media-projected-on-forms/README.md) asked for before anything in step 2 of [one world, many cameras](../../../docs/design/one-world-many-cameras.md) is built on its deductions 2 and 3. Face rules under test: [DDR-0004](../../../docs/decisions/ddr/0004-entity-form-with-media-faces-derived.md) (proposed). Renderer rules: [ADR-0019](../../../docs/decisions/adr/0019-three-js-for-the-field-ground-in-a-flagged-spike.md) (proposed). The scene core it reuses was tested in [EXP-0013](../0013-scene-core-on-webgpu/README.md).

## Hypothesis

Four claims, each of which can fail on its own:

1. **Media on faces.** Each entity's form shows its cover in front and its other pictures round the sides, top and bottom in `media[]` order, blank faces plain, all from one `DataArrayTexture` read by six per-instance layer numbers. One draw call for all forms.
2. **One system for box and sphere.** The sphere is the same rest cube deformed in the vertex stage (`normalize(tan(c · π/4))`), and the colour reads the rest cube's face and uv, so a picture stays on the same surface point through the whole morph. Per-form shapes come from a per-instance value, not a second mesh.
3. **Both backends.** The same TSL source looks the same on WebGPU and on `?gl=webgl2`: same face order, no flip or mirror, same filtering.
4. **Animation, first try.** Each form turns a quarter turn about its vertical axis, eased, after a hold, with a phase per form so neighbours do not turn in step. Within a few form-widths of the camera it eases round to face the camera along the short way (DDR-0004: the animation should settle or face the visitor on approach).

## Method

Code: `apps/web/app/lab/forms/page.tsx` (route, `noindex`) and the feature folder `apps/web/components/forms-lab/`:

- `faces.ts`: DDR-0004 derivation from the entity's `FieldObject`. Cover is `thumb`, else the first picture (a video counts with its poster); the rest follow in slot order front, right, back, left, top, bottom, repeating until all six faces are covered; more than six are dropped; drawings and documents are contained, photographs cover-cropped at their `focal` point.
- `layers.ts`: one 256² RGBA8 layer per distinct picture, at most 256 layers (WebGL 2's floor, WebGPU's default). Layers 0 to 5 are test cards (slot name, an arrow to the face's up, a dot in the uv origin corner). Pictures arrive six at a time and each is uploaded alone with `addLayerUpdate`. Mipmaps generated by three.
- `form.ts`: `BoxGeometry(2, 2, 2, 16, 16, 16)` with a `faceSlot` attribute from its normals; three `vec4` instanced attributes (layers, shape and phase, position and proportion). `positionNode` builds the whole transform from `positionGeometry`, because in r187 it replaces `positionLocal` after the instancing transform; the instance matrix stays identity and `frustumCulled` is off. Proportion from the cover's aspect (DDR-0004), applied to the sphere as well, so a tall cover gives an ellipsoid.
- `scene.ts`: `WebGPURenderer` (`?gl=webgl2` forces WebGL 2), orbit camera, a faint grid, the controls.

Steps, by eye, in Chrome and Safari 26, first on the default backend and then on `?gl=webgl2`:

1. Test cards on: each face reads its slot name upright from outside, arrow up on the four sides, the dot bottom left. Top and bottom: note which way they point.
2. Test cards off: covers in front, other pictures round the form, every face covered, drawings letterboxed.
3. Slider cube to sphere: pictures stay put; the sphere's poles and the eight cube corners are where the stretch shows.
4. Per-form shape on: cubes, half-way forms and spheres at once, still one draw call.
5. Turn and settle: walk the camera toward a form and away. Does it face you in time? Does the field read as calm or busy?
6. Status line: draw count against form count, picture load count, backend name.

## Observations

**2026-11-06, built** (branch `exp/0006-spaces`).

- Type-check, lint and `pnpm build` pass; `/lab/forms` is prerendered with every entity's face list baked in at build time, and three stays in a lazy chunk.
- Source finding: three r187's GLSL builder makes a varying `flat` only when its type is integer and ignores `setInterpolation("flat")` on a float; the WGSL builder honours it. The face's layer is therefore interpolated on WebGL 2, constant per triangle but possibly a hair off the integer, so the shader rounds it instead of truncating.
- Source finding: `material.positionNode` runs after three's own instancing transform, so a deformation written from `positionLocal` would be applied to already-placed vertices. Built from `positionGeometry` instead, with all per-instance values in attributes.
- Not yet checked: anything in a browser.

Session notes go below, one dated entry per session.

- First look (2026-11-06, owner, WebGPU). Many faces black. Cause: three r187 uploads only the layers in `layerUpdates` whenever the set is non-empty, including the texture's first upload; the six test cards were listed before the first render, so every other layer reached the GPU as zeros and stayed black until its own picture arrived (and for good if it failed). Fixed in `layers.ts`: layers are listed only after `onUpdate` reports the first full upload. Grey faces were the blank-face rule: plain material where an entity has fewer pictures than faces.
- Owner, same look: the form should be covered entirely by its media, even with one picture. DDR-0004 counts rule revised (still proposed); `faces.ts` now repeats pictures in order to fill all six faces.
- Second look (owner): status `149/149 pictures · 155 layers · 3 draws`, yet many forms stayed plain. The console showed CORS failures on `media.amorf.co`, although `curl` with an `Origin` header got `access-control-allow-origin` on all 169 face pictures. Likely cause: the field had loaded the same files as plain `<img>` without CORS, and Chrome reused those cached responses, which have no CORS header. The lab now fetches each picture with `cache: "reload"` and decodes it with `createImageBitmap`. The lasting fix is the CORS and cache rule in `docs/deployment.md`, which the field's forms will need in step 2. Three draws for 34 forms, not one: not yet explained.
- Owner request (2026-11-06): the forms should feel like they float, turning about all axes. Added a float layer to the same vertex stage, toggled by "float (all axes)": per axis two sines at unrelated slow frequencies seeded by the form's phase (pitch up to about 0.32 rad, roll 0.28, a yaw wobble 0.14 on top of the quarter-turn cadence), a bob of 0.18, a sway of 0.16 and a constant hover of 0.3 above the ground. It fades to 25 % within settle range of the camera and to 0 in the entity view (EXP-0016), so a form near you or open lies flat and faces you. Not yet judged by eye; amplitudes and frequencies are first guesses. A full tumble was not tried, because it would turn the cover away from the visitor at random. Every number is now a row of a motion matrix panel in the lab (amplitude and speed per channel; spread between forms, calm near the camera, turn speed, hold), with reset and copy-as-JSON, so the owner tunes by hand; the chosen values then go into `DEFAULT_MOTION` in `form.ts`. Each channel runs on its own CPU clock (dt · speed), so a speed change never jumps the phase.
