# EXP-0009: Circuit against cul-de-sac as the topology of a space

Follows [STU-0001](../../studies/0001-promenade-architecturale/README.md). Depends on [EXP-0006](../0006-thresholds-between-spaces/README.md) (spaces must be felt) and on journeys being recorded.

## Hypothesis

Le Corbusier's two promenade houses take opposite shapes: Maison La Roche splits into two paths that each end in a cul-de-sac, Villa Savoye climbs a ramp into a circuit that closes on the roof (STU-0001, notes on the houses). If topology is a property of the space, the same entities arranged two ways should leave visibly different journeys.

It is wrong if the journey tracks look alike.
