# EXP-0000: Short title naming the question

Copy this folder to `NNNN-kebab-title/` and delete these instructions. Rules: [../../../docs/conventions/research-log.md](../../../docs/conventions/research-log.md).

## Hypothesis

What you expect, and why. Say what result would show you wrong.

## Method

What you did or will do, in enough detail to repeat it: materials, tools, steps, conditions.

## Observations

What happened, in order, including what failed. Failed attempts and trade-offs stay here; they are part of the record.

## Outcome

The answer to the question, or the reason the experiment stopped (an abandoned experiment is a valid result). Name the decision record it backs, if any.

## Next

What this opens: a follow-up experiment, a decision record to write, or nothing.
