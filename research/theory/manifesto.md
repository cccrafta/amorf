# Manifesto (draft)

This is a working statement of what amorf claims. It is a draft: wording, scope and order are open.

## Structure as primary ornamentation

Ornament is not added to structure. Structural necessity, material properties, layout logic and honest joinery are the ornament. What holds a thing up, how its material behaves, how its parts meet and why they are laid out as they are already carry the form; where a feature exists only to decorate, the project asks what it is hiding.

The same applies to the work itself. The app's grid, its fixed frame and its plain print look are not styling over the material; they are its structure, shown.

## Process over final artifact

The finished artifact is one output. The research behind it, including failed experiments, abandoned directions and trade-offs, is kept and shown, because the reasoning is part of the result and the failures are how the result was reached.

## Decisions backed by recorded reasoning

A choice of technology, form or material is written down with its context, the options considered and the reasons, and linked to the experiments that support it. A decision that changes is superseded by a new record; the old one stays.

## Questions this raises

- What counts as structure in a digital work, and what is its ornament?
- How much of the process can be shown without burying the artifact?
- How do material properties of physical references (mud-brick, concrete, timber) become constraints on an interface?

Each question that can be tested becomes an experiment in [../experiments/](../experiments/).
