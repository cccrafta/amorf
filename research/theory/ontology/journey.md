# Journey

A part of the [ontology](README.md). This page is the hub for the part: what it is, what the project currently holds about it, and the research filed under it. The studies and experiments stay the source; this page links to them.

## Definition

The recorded path of discovery. Entities are nodes; edges are the visitor's sequence of moves between them. A visitor can look back at the journey of a session. A journey can be organic (as walked) or tailored (nodes placed in or taken out afterwards). Because entities have coordinates, a journey is movement between coordinates and can be drawn as a map. A journey is a collection of entities with a sequence, so it is itself a space: a space of discovery. This is where the temporal experience of discovery is recorded.

Architectural reading: Spatial sequence: the footprint left by the walk.
