# Journey

A part of the [ontology](README.md). This page is the hub for the part: what it is, what the project currently holds about it, and the research filed under it. The studies and experiments stay the source; this page links to them.

## Definition

The recorded path of discovery. Entities are nodes; edges are the visitor's sequence of moves between them. A visitor can look back at the journey of a session. A journey can be organic (as walked) or tailored (nodes placed in or taken out afterwards). Because entities have coordinates, a journey is movement between coordinates and can be drawn as a map. A journey is a collection of entities with a sequence, so it is itself a space: a space of discovery. This is where the temporal experience of discovery is recorded.

Architectural reading: Spatial sequence: the footprint left by the walk.

## Position

Deductions currently held, newest last. Each entry is dated and cites its evidence. Never rewrite an entry; add a new one that revises it.

- **2026-11-01:** Defined as above in the ontology conversation ([transcript](transcript.md)).
- **2026-11-04:** A journey is the visitor's sequence of entity views: the space it was walked in plus the entity ids in order, with times. It has no coordinates of its own; its map is drawn from that space's placements. Camera views (EXP-0010) are not part of it unless that experiment earns them. Source: owner statement, recorded in [DDR-0003](../../../docs/decisions/ddr/0003-placements-in-plan-units-per-space.md) (proposed).
- **2026-11-05:** A journey can be drawn on the ground of the space it crossed, as a trace in the place itself, not only as an overlay ([STU-0003](../../studies/0003-place-not-void/README.md), deduction 9).
