# STU-0000: Short title naming the subject studied

## Question

What you want to understand, and which part of the ontology it serves.

## Sources

Works, buildings, precedents and people consulted, with enough detail to find them again. Mark what has been read and what is still to read.

## Notes

What the sources say, quoted or summarised and attributed. Keep the sources' claims apart from your own reading of them.

## Deductions

What you conclude for amorf, each traceable to a note above. A deduction worth holding is copied, dated, into the Position section of each part hub it concerns.
